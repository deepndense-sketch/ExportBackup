Backup Project - one-time update repair

1. Close Adobe Premiere Pro.
2. Extract this ZIP into a folder.
3. Double-click repair-update.cmd. Keep repair-update.ps1 beside it.
4. Wait for "Installed and verified". Internet access is required.
5. Open Premiere Pro and Backup Project.

Future updates use the plugin's Update button.
The repair keeps copies of duplicate installations outside the CEP folder.
If repair fails, keep the window's error message and contact the plugin author.
